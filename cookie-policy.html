Cookies on This Website

This page describes the limited cookie currently used by the News Imex International website.

Current Cookie

When you acknowledge our cookie notice, the website stores a small first-party cookie named newsimex_cookies. It remembers that acknowledgement so the notice does not repeatedly appear.

No Analytics Cookies Currently

The current website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics or other non-essential tracking is introduced later, this policy and the website's consent controls should be updated before that tracking is enabled.

Managing Cookies

You can remove or block cookies using your browser settings. Removing the acknowledgement cookie may cause the cookie notice to appear again on a future visit.
