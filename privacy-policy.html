Your Privacy Matters

This policy explains how News Imex International handles information submitted through this website.

When you contact us or request a quote, we may collect your name, company, email address, phone number, product or service requirements, destination, quantity, budget, shipping preference and any additional information you choose to provide.

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, communicate about requested trade services, maintain business records and improve customer service. We do not sell personal information.

Website submissions may be stored in a restricted Google Sheet and sent to our business Gmail account. Google may process this information as a service provider according to its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

We may share information with suppliers, logistics providers or other service partners only when reasonably necessary to respond to your request or provide requested services, or when required by law.

Retention & Security

We retain inquiry information only as reasonably needed for business, legal and service purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you submitted or request correction or deletion where applicable. Email Newsimexinternational@gmail.com.
